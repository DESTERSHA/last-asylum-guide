Squads

Squads 1–4: who goes where

A squad is 5 heroes and their troops. Squad strength = the right heroes + the right positions + the one-faction bonus. The same heroes can win or lose — it all depends on who stands where.

Formation

Who stands in front, who behind — and why

In the battle pictures below, labels look like «1-3»: the first number is the squad (1 — yours, 2 — the enemy), the second is the hero's slot in this layout.

How a battle goes — three phases

1
Start: they hit the tanks

The enemy splits into two groups. Its slots 1, 3 and 4 hit your slot 1, and slots 2 and 5 hit your slot 2. So three hit slot 1 and two hit slot 2.

2
One tank falls — everyone hits the second

This is the turning point: all five enemies switch to the remaining tank.

3
Both tanks fall — they hit the back row

Usually that is the end of the battle.

Takeaway: put the toughest tank (Arthur) in slot 1. A tank in the back protects nobody, and a fragile damage hero in front dies first.

One faction

The bonus for heroes of one faction

If a squad has several heroes of one faction (Warriors, Rangers or Warlocks), the whole squad gets a bonus to attack, HP and defense:

Who is in the squadBonus
3 heroes of one faction+5%
3 of one + 2 of another faction+10%
4 heroes of one faction+15%
All 5 of one faction+20%

The bonus doesn't turn on right away — only after the first «Royal City Scramble» in the kingdom. That is what the squad window says.

Should you take an «outside» fifth hero?

If the fifth hero of your faction is much weaker (few stars, poor skills, no gear) and the «outside» one is strong — keep the outside one. 4 of yours + 1 outside gives +15%, and that is fine.

Switch to all five of one faction when the fifth hero holds their role properly. Later, Raven Epigraphs that work for only one faction will make such a squad even stronger.

How to check: don't look at the Might number. Swap the hero and compare the result: the same arena opponent, the same boss damage.

Who beats whom

Warriors

Stronger than Rangers, weaker than Warlocks.

Arthur, Marlena, Harper, Daskal, Bella, Claire…

Warlocks

Stronger than Warriors, weaker than Rangers.

Ulfrid, Annie, Joker, Grenwald, Stellar, Hastar…

Rangers

Stronger than Warlocks, weaker than Warriors.

Shadow, Cynthia, Red Lady, Louis, Bell, Griffith…

«Stronger» means: the hero takes 20% less damage from the faction it beats. That is what the Arena hint in the game says: «Warriors take 20% less damage from Rangers», «Rangers — from Warlocks», «Warlocks — from Warriors». So before attacking a player, check their squad's faction and send those who are strong against it.

SQUAD RESEARCH

Each squad has its own branch

In the Research Lab squads have their own tiles — «Squad 1», «Squad 2», «Squad 3» (frames); the percentage on a tile is how much of the branch is already researched.

Squad 1 and 4 branches are cheap — level them. Branch 2 costs 15 times more, branch 3 — 76 times more: take only defense and HP there.

SQUAD ON THE WALL

«Configure City Defense»

Tap the city Walls. The window lists your squads in order: they fight the attacker in order 1–4 (the arrows on the right change the order). The Join Defense checkbox (frame) — the squad defends the city.

Before the raid day in the Duel (Saturday), untick it for strong squads: if your shield drops, you lose less. For the Undead Siege — tick it back.

PvE

Against monsters (Blight)

1
All five of one faction

+20% to everything. Monsters are not «stronger» against any faction, so a one-faction squad is the best choice here.

2
Add heroes who are strong against monsters

Claire (+15% damage to monsters for the three strongest Warriors), Celia (damage to monsters and more resources), Grenwald, Stellar. Bella and Lucius reduce monster damage to your heroes.

3
Wear purple gear

Purple items get a bonus against monsters at levels 10, 20 and 30. Details — in the Gear section.

4
Don't go above your strength

Tap a monster on the map. At the bottom of the window is «Rec. Might»: hit those whose value is below your squad's Might. Above it are «Guaranteed» (what drops for sure) and «Potential Rewards», at the bottom right — how much Stamina a hit costs (lightning). To hit — the round Attack button under the monster.

World Boss

Against the Demon King

The boss doesn't hit back, so you need no defense — only damage. The daily ranking is by your best single hit.

1
Remove the tanks

4 damage heroes + 1 support, or 3 damage + 2 supports that buff several heroes at once.

2
Damage in front, support behind

This is a starting point. Then try moving heroes one at a time and write down the damage.

3
The faction of the day hits harder

Mon and Thu — Warriors, Tue and Fri — Warlocks, Wed and Sat — Rangers. The game doesn't say exactly how much harder. But if a hero of the right faction is much weaker — take your leveled one.

4
Purple level 30 gauntlets on the main damage heroes

They give about 6% damage to monsters and often hit harder than gold ones.

5
Scout any target before hitting

«War Frenzy» turns on — the hit gets stronger.

Example of a strong combo: Marlena, Claire, Cynthia, Harper, Bell. Harper raises everyone's attack and Warriors' even more, Bell — the two strongest and all Rangers, Claire — damage to monsters for the three strongest Warriors. Marlena gets 4 buffs at once.

Watch the order: if Marlena hits before Harper gives the buff, the damage is lower.

The «Black Raven Fortress» Sanctuary skin: just owning it gives +1% damage to monsters, wearing it — another +5%.

Of the purple heroes, for the boss it is only worth leveling the skills of Ash (boss damage) and Celia (resources).

PvP

Against players and in the arena

1
Check the enemy's faction before attacking

Against Warriors send Warlocks, against Warlocks — Rangers, against Rangers — Warriors.

2
3 of one faction + 2 of another — a flexible option

Gives +10% and lets you avoid losing outright to any faction. If the enemy doesn't counter your faction — 4 + 1 (+15%).

3
Tanks in front, always

Hastar, Stellar, Ulfrid (reflects damage) are worth more in fights with players than against monsters.

4
Gold gear

Against players you need the pure stats of gold items, not the anti-monster bonus.

Arena

  • The Arena building — from Sanctuary 7. Arena battles don't kill troops — you only risk your ranking place.
  • Regular arena: 5 free battles and 3 free list refreshes a day.
  • Collect placement rewards by hand every day — the next day they are gone.
  • Squad 1 is your arena defense. Put your best there.
  • Higher arenas unlock by your place in the kingdom Might ranking.
  • Where 3 defense squads are needed, spread the strength evenly: the enemy only needs to win 2 battles of 3.
