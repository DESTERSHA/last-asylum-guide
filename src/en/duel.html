Alliance Duel: what to do on each day

Every week our alliance competes with another alliance. Six days — six themes. Points are given only for the day's theme. Whoever saves up in advance and spends on the right day wins.

The key skill

How to save up: two tricks that win the Duel

«Saving» Falcon Quests — what it is and how

Falcon Quests are short quests from the Falcon Tower: they hang on its map as colored markers (a skull — hit Blight, a hand — help an ally, a gift — collect a reward, etc.). Once done, a marker waits until you tap «Claim All».

«Saving» means: complete the quest but DON'T tap «Claim». The completed quest stays in the tower «pending». You claim it on the right day — and only then the game gives Duel points.

  1. On Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday you do Falcon Quests and don't claim the reward.
  2. On Monday, Wednesday and Friday you go to the tower and claim everything — 13,000 points each.
  3. On Saturday you do and claim right away.
How many you can save. New quests appear every 6 hours, but only a limited number can stay «pending» — that is your Falcon level. At the top right next to the question mark you see «waiting / max». At the top left is a compass with a number — that is the Falcon level. Tap the compass to see how many quests are left until the next level.

If the limit is full, new quests won't appear — better claim the extras so they aren't wasted.

«Holding the hammer» — what it is and how

When construction finishes, a hammer appears above the building. Until you tap it, the upgrade is not counted.

Duel points are given for tapping the hammer, not for the end of construction. So:

  1. Start a long construction in advance (on Sunday or Monday).
  2. Construction finished — don't tap the hammer. The building queue is freed up anyway, you can keep building.
  3. On Tuesday (building day) tap all the hammers — you get points for everything at once.

Same with research: collect finished research on Wednesday or Friday.

Double benefit

Combine the Duel with the Survival Battle

The Survival Battle runs every day. The day is split into 4-hour rounds, each with its own theme: «Raven Enhancement», «Enhance Hero», «Build Territory», «Train Soldiers», «Technology Research». The button is in «Special Events» at the top right.

If you spend the Duel day's items in the 4-hour round with the matching theme, the points go to two events at once.

Example: Monday is Raven day. Open the Survival Battle, see when the «Raven Enhancement» round starts — and feed the Raven exactly then.

Careful: if you do all Survival Battle quests every day without thinking, nothing will be left for the Duel. The Duel comes first; fit the Battle around it.

Duel research — do this first

The Lab has an «Alliance Duel» branch. It opens at Lab level 15 and is leveled with Study Scrolls. Without it you only get the first 3 chests of 9.

1
First the «Super Rewards» node — unlocks level 4–6 rewards

Costs about 100 scrolls. Every following Duel pays more right away.

2
Then «Top Rewards» — unlocks level 7–9 rewards

About 450 scrolls.

3
Then «Arena Expert» — to the max

It increases all Duel points except Diamond purchases; at max — double. About 3,800 scrolls.

4
The rest of the branch

Separate nodes: «Falcon Points», «Speedup Points», «Recruitment Points», «Building Points», «Tech Points», «Training Points», «Kill Points», «Caravan Trade Points», «Covert Operation Points», «Survivor Recruitment Points».

Study Scrolls come from the Duel's own chests — it's a loop: more research → more points → more chests → more scrolls.

For leaders

How to lead the alliance in the Duel

The plan on Sunday

Post in the alliance announcement what to save and when to spend it (the table above). Without it, newcomers burn speedups on the wrong day.

You don't have to win every day

Each day gives its own victory points. If a day is hopeless — let everyone save for the next one instead of wasting their stock.

Check everyone's research

Whoever is below Lab 15 or lacks the chest nodes — help them with alliance help speedups.
