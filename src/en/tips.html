Non-obvious

Tricks that give you an edge

Free Sanctuary relocation in Kingdom War

Moving between kingdoms doesn't use up items: the game says «This cross-kingdom relocation will not consume Advanced Relocates». Need to move to another spot on your own map — don't spend a «Direct Relocate» or Diamonds. Move to the enemy kingdom, and from there straight back home to the spot you need. There is a 5-minute cooldown between moves. It works when moving to the enemy kingdom is open (Saturday from 05:05 Moscow for attackers). Details — in the Kingdom War section.

Scout before the boss

Before hitting the Demon King, scout any target. «War Frenzy» turns on — check that it is lit, then hit.

The Demon King shortens the trip

If a squad is on a long march far away, hitting the Demon King makes the trip there and back no longer than 30 seconds.

Purple gauntlets for the boss

Purple level 30 gauntlets give +6% damage to monsters and are often stronger than gold ones on the boss. Don't dismantle two pairs. Why.

Double points

Spend the Duel day's resources in the 4 hours of the Survival Battle when the theme matches. The points go to both events at once.

Gold — on Tuesday

A gold caravan = 100,000 Duel points (like 2,000 minutes of building speedups). A gold Covert Operation — 75,000.

Raven chests — on Wednesday

A Lv.7 Raven Gear Chest on Wednesday = 810,000 Duel points.

Minister title — before you start

A title only speeds up what is started after you get it. First get the title, then start building.

The «Black Raven Fortress» skin

+1% damage to monsters just for owning it, and +5% if it is worn.

The Fame Medal shop

Buy only gear blueprints. Curio Chests and universal shards there are a bad deal.

Arena — rewards by hand

Arena battles don't cost troops. Collect placement rewards every day — the next day they are gone.

The «Alliance Duel» branch

A separate tile in the Lab. It increases Duel points and unlocks the next Duel reward chests — level it as soon as it appears.

Stamina: how not to lose it

Stamina regenerates by itself at 1 unit every 5 minutes («every 300 s»). With a flask you can raise it above the maximum (for example, to 170 with a max of 120), so you can drink the free flask even when the bar is full.

1
Tap the Stamina bar

The green bar with a lightning bolt at the top left, under the portrait. The «Restore Stamina» window opens: at the top how much Stamina you have now, below — ways to refill it.

2
The top row — 50 free Stamina

«Left Today: 0/2» — how many of the two free flasks you can still take today. While a flask is not ready, a gray timer replaces the button; the next one comes 4 hours after the previous.

3
Tap as soon as it is available

Unused free flasks burn at the end of the game day (05:00 Moscow).

4
Other rows — for Diamonds and from your bag

The yellow button — buy 50 Stamina for 150 Diamonds (a bad deal). The blue «Use» buttons — drink flasks you already have (10 and 50 Stamina, «Owned» shows how many). The box with a number next to it — how many flasks to drink at once.

Buildings

Which buildings to upgrade first

1
Sanctuary — always first

No building can be higher than it, and it sets how far heroes can level. The upgrade window says so: «Max hero level +5».

2
Read the Sanctuary upgrade window

At the bottom — what the next level needs: buildings (red — what is missing, the «Go» button takes you there) and resources (the «Get» button). That is your to-do list.

3
Lab — together with the Sanctuary

Most Sanctuary upgrades need it. Start it in the same hour as the Sanctuary — then neither timer waits for the other.

4
Builder's Hut

Gives a free speedup to every construction. Especially valuable before Sanctuary 15 — keep it at the Sanctuary's level.

5
Herb Garden — first among resource buildings

Herb is the main shortage: building, research and gear need it.

6
Smelting Workshop — to level 25 early

It gives Gearstone.

Troops

1
One Training Grounds at max, three at level 10

Three cheap Training Grounds quickly train mid-tier troops (T4), and then you promote them to the best. This saves 6–7 hours per cycle.

2
Promote, don't train from scratch

Turn old weak troops into strong ones — cheaper and faster.

Heal on time, especially before Kingdom War.

The Demon King, caravans, Elixir Scramble and the arena — almost no troop losses. These are safe rewards.

Diamonds and purchases

Where to spend Diamonds and what to buy

PurchaseWorth it?
1. VIPYes, first of all. VIP gives permanent bonuses to heroes' attack, defense and HP, building, research, training, healing and gathering. Details — below.
2. Wishing WheelYes, second. Spin the wheel at the end of the event up to the guaranteed hero — that's how people get Cynthia and other gold heroes.
3. Hero recruitingYes. Free attempts every day on a timer, Diamonds — once VIP and the wheel are covered.
«Targeted relocate»Not for Diamonds. It is given for free (event rewards) and sold in the Alliance Shop for alliance points — get it there.

Stamina for DiamondsOnly if you really need it for an event.

If you pay money — what's worth it. The Monthly Pass (VIP for 30 days, bonuses to building, gathering, attack and defense, a fourth squad). The daily pack with gear and Raven Essence. The first purchase — Marlena is in it.

What's not worth it. Buying «Diamonds» in the shop — the worst price there. And the «Packs» section without thinking.

VIP and Survivors

VIP and Survivors — what they give

VIP

VIP points build up by themselves: every day for logging in, and the more days in a row you log in, the more points («Logged in for N days in a row» in the VIP window). Miss a day — the streak starts over.

Level bonuses work while VIP time is running («Remaining VIP Time» — extended with items and purchases).

  • heroes' HP, attack and defense, march speed;
  • Grain, Timber, Herb production; building, research, training and healing speed; barracks capacity;
  • VIP 6 — auto-dispatch of Covert Operations, VIP 8 — quick summon in the Expedition Battle, VIP 12 — Covert Operations super mode;
  • each level has its own free daily pack: collect it every day.

Survivors

Survivors are residents you hire and send to work in buildings (the «Send» / «Quick Dispatch» button in the «Survivor List»). Each has a post: Farmer, Lumberjack, Herbalist — more resources; Builder — a free building speedup; Explorer, Researcher — research; Instructor, Officer — troops; Nurse — healing; Blacksmith — gear; storage keepers — resource protection.

The higher a Survivor's rarity (UR, SSR) and stars, the stronger the bonus. Stars are raised with shards of the same Survivor.

Two special UR Survivors: the bounty hunter Freya — with her Falcon Quests give extra reward chests; Shadow Mercenary Elaine. Neither can be pulled from a regular random UR chest.

Arenas and Curios

Arenas: regular and Triangular

In all arenas troops don't die — battles use the highest-tier troops of your territory. The rules below are a short summary of the rules window in the game itself.

Rookie Arena

Registration is automatic. You challenge opponents of a similar rank; beat someone ranked higher and you swap places. You are defended by Squad 1 (the defense line-up can be changed). Ranking rewards come by mail once a week.

Apex Arena and Arena of Fate

A week long, from Monday 01:00 server time. Start — 1000 points, each challenge offers 5 opponents to choose from: a win adds points, a loss takes them away. 1, 3 and 5 challenges a day give daily rewards. The Arena of Fate admits by Hero Might: top 200 of the kingdom — advanced, 201–1200 — intermediate.

Triangular Arena

Only for the top 200 by Hero Might in the kingdom. You build 3 teams, the battle goes to two wins — each side's three teams fight in turn. Attack and defense formations are saved separately. 30 attempts a day. Every day you can like three of the top 3 — 30 Diamonds per like.

Tip for the Triangular Arena: split your strength evenly between three teams — the enemy only needs to win two of three. One super team and two weak ones lose.

Curios

Curio Hall

Curios are special decorative buildings in the city. The game says: «Collect and activate Curios to activate their stats. The higher the Curio quality, the more stats it gives».

Each active Curio gives bonuses to attack, defense, special stats and economy and raises the Might of all heroes at once.

How to level

  • A Curio is upgraded with its own shards or universal Curio shards (not for every Curio — the game will warn you).
  • Extra shards can be exchanged for a whole Curio («Curio Exchange»).
  • You can place a limited number («Building: N/M»). Put a Curio into storage — its bonus disappears.
Beginner mistakes

What not to do

Upgrading buildings for Might

The number grows, but the army doesn't. Battles are decided by heroes, troops, research.

Spending everything as soon as you get it

The same resources on their Duel day would have brought the alliance a win.

Playing without an alliance

No help, gifts, Alliance Shop. From level 7 the Sanctuary needs an Alliance Hall.

No shield overnight

A city with full storage and no shield is the first target for raids.

Stars for Marlena before Arthur

The tank must survive first, otherwise Marlena won't get to hit.

Gearstone into a tank's sword

A tank doesn't need a sword. Gearstone goes into the damage hero's weapon.
