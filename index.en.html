For beginners

Just started? Do this in order

Six steps in order. Tap a screenshot to see it full screen.

STEP 1 · on your very first day

Join an alliance

The Alliance button is at the bottom right of the screen, in the column above Mail. The Join Alliance window opens.

Find ours by the tag in square brackets (you will be given the tag) and tap Apply. If the alliance is open, the button says Join.

The alliance speeds up building, gives gifts, its own shop and help in battle.

STEP 2

Skim the glossary

Five minutes — and you understand what people write in chat: Rally, Garrison, Blight, tile, "zeroed".

Open the glossary — it has search.

STEP 3

Build your first squad

2 tanks in front, 3 heroes behind, ideally all of one faction — the game gives a bonus to attack, HP and defense for that.

Free start: Arthur, Bella, Marlena (if you have her), Claire, Celia. Squads.

STEP 4

Upgrade the Sanctuary first

The Sanctuary sets the cap for everything: hero, building and troop levels. Upgrade the Lab together with it.

Which buildings to upgrade and in what order.

STEP 5 · every day

10 minutes of must-dos

10 hits on the Demon King, 4 raids on other players' caravans, all building and research queues busy, Falcon sent out.

Checklist — the checkmarks are remembered on your phone.

STEP 6

Don't spend everything at once

Save speedups, Antitoxin and Raven fruit and spend them strictly on their Duel day: the alliance gets points for the same thing. Which day is for what.

And on Friday evening — heal your troops and bring squads home: on Saturday it's Kingdom War.
